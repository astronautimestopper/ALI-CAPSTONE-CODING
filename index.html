The main rules of the game

The main goal of Uno is to be the first player to get rid of all your cards. This can be done by matching your cards with the same number, colour or symbol as the card that is played by your opponent

Types of Cards

There are many types of cards in Uno. They include: Number cards - 1,2,3 etc.
Symbol cards - Reverse, Block, etc.
And the all powerful Joker Card.
